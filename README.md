### 📈 Featured Project

**Irish Outpatient Waiting List Analysis (2021–2026)**  
*Microsoft Excel | Power Query | PivotTables | XLOOKUP | Data Visualisation*

Analysed 4,895 hospital-level records from Ireland's National Treatment Purchase Fund (NTPF), covering outpatient waiting lists between April 2021 and August 2026.

- Cleaned, validated and combined multiple datasets using Excel and Power Query.
- Applied descriptive statistics, hospital-level comparisons and regional analysis to identify trends.
- Used XLOOKUP to standardise and enrich hospital data with geographic classifications.
- Developed an interactive Excel dashboard featuring KPIs, charts and slicers.
- Identified a **9.13% increase in outpatient waiting-list volumes**, alongside a **21.25-percentage-point reduction** in the proportion of patients waiting over 18 months.
